Privacy notice — Magic Library

Game ID 2113708279749918721
Updated 24 October 2026

This notice is published by the developer of Magic Library. It covers players of the game, including players in the United Kingdom.

What the game does not ask for

Magic Library has no account. It does not ask for, and does not send the developer, your name, phone number, email address, identification number, location, photos, microphone recordings, camera images, contacts, or payment details.

What stays on your device

The game may save the following settings in local storage on the device or browser you are using. The game does not upload them:

  • the language you chose
  • music and sound settings
  • look sensitivity and the viewpoint height
  • whether candle lighting is switched on

Clearing the game’s stored data, or the browser storage for the game, removes these settings. Removing the game from the device removes them as well.

The place where you open the game

If you open Magic Library inside another app or store, that platform may process information under its own notice, for example for its own account, advertising, or payments. That is the platform’s processing. This game does not run its own advertising and does not take payment.

Age

The game is offered with an 8+ age rating. It does not ask for a date of birth or an identity document.
